Courses and workshops

In my workshops people learn by getting involved: activities, games and discussion instead of lectures. I work on inclusion, diversity and active citizenship with students, volunteers and fellow trainers.

How I work

My workshops follow the experiential learning cycle: first we live a concrete activity, often a game or a sport, then we stop and make sense of it together.

  1. Do

    A game, a sports challenge, a story to build: something to experience first-hand.

  2. Observe

    We pause to look at what happened, how we felt and what worked.

  3. Name it

    From what we observed we draw out the key ideas: stereotypes, inclusion, cooperation.

  4. Try again

    We go back to the activity with fresh eyes, or carry what we learnt into everyday life.
