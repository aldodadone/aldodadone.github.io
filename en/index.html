About me

I live in Rome and have too many interests. You’ve been warned. Since 2018 I have been handling communication for organisations working on human rights, inclusion and educational poverty. Along the way I became a trainer, and today I bring the two together.

I believe design can make a difference, and that a story told well is already a small change.
