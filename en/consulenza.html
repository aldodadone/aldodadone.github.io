Services

If you represent an institution, an organisation or a school, I can support you with training, communication and project management. But first, I listen: every proposal starts from your needs.

What I offer

Skills identification and validation

I help you recognise and describe the skills you have gained through work, volunteering or study, leading to a clear, well-evidenced skills portfolio. I hold the Lazio Region qualifications of Skills Identifier and Evidence Assessor, and when the process leads to certification I work with accredited bodies.

Who it’s for

  • People with experience to showcase
  • Training providers

Learning design

I design tailored non-formal education programmes and workshops: learning objectives, activities, materials and evaluation.

Who it’s for

  • Schools
  • Associations
  • Training providers
  • European partnerships

Inclusion and diversity in organisations

Training and support to make language, communication and welcome more inclusive, with a particular focus on SOGIESC and intercultural dialogue.

Who it’s for

  • Associations
  • Public services
  • Companies
  • Volunteer groups

Communication for non-profits

Strategy, editorial plan, visual identity and campaigns, so the people who need you can understand you.

Who it’s for

  • Associations
  • Cooperatives
  • Committees
  • Cultural projects

Project management

Support in planning and coordinating social, cultural and European projects: timelines, resources, risks, reporting. My training includes UC Irvine’s project management specialisation, a 64-hour course on the ISIPM model and the ISO 21502 standard, and a professional diploma in project management.

Who it’s for

  • Non-profit organisations
  • Groups applying for funding

How it works

  1. You write to me

    Tell me in a few lines who you are and what you need.

  2. We talk

    We set up a meeting, online if you like, to understand goals, timelines and resources.

  3. I propose a plan

    You receive a clear proposal with activities, timelines and costs.

  4. We work together

    We keep each other updated along the way and adjust course when needed.

Tell me what you need

The first step is an email: you’ll find all the ways to reach me on the contact page.
